Who we are

A small studio with a big drawing table.

aqyr studios was founded to make thoughtful architecture at every scale — from a single family home to a building used by a whole neighbourhood.

We work closely with clients, builders and specialists, keeping the team small so the people who draw your project are the ones who see it built.
